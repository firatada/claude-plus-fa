# claude-plus-fa: dictation in the answer boxes

What is true now; history in [`trials.md`](../trials.md). Moved here whole from [`reply.md`](./reply.md) on 2026-11-25, when that file reached its 50,000-character budget.

## Scope
- Owns: Space held in an answer box, both paths (Claude, Whisper), the voice host, the notes, the dictation settings in `claude-plus.local.json`, `voice.log`, and their limits.
- Not here: the answer boxes themselves, their keys, drafts and what Send to Session pastes ([`reply.md`](./reply.md)); the panel's other windows ([`memory.md`](../memory.md)).

## Dictation in an answer box (Space held)
- Two paths, one take each: **Claude** (default since 2026-11-25): the panel's hidden Claude Code does the take with its own hold-to-talk (§ Voice host); **Whisper** (the fallback): the page records and `agent-reply.py` `Dictation` sends to the maintainer's local Voice to Text server (since 2026-11-23; Windows' Win+H typed nothing on this PC). Setting `"dictation"` in `claude-plus.local.json`: `"whisper"` = Voice to Text alone (the hidden Claude Code stopped at the next live pass); anything else or missing = Claude (`dictation_mode()`, read at every hold and live pass). The page records every take either way (the level meter, and the sound should the take go to Voice to Text); nothing is stored, the sound lives in the page's memory for the hold. The rest of this section is the Whisper path, unless it says otherwise.
- Hold rule `spaceRule(s, ev)` (pure, run in node by `test-reply.py`), Claude Code 2.1.330's own hold-to-talk read from its binary (`trials.md`, 2026-11-23): a plain Space keydown (no modifier, not composing; `preventDefault`) types one space at once (`execCommand("insertText")`, undo and `input` work) and starts `HOLD_MS` 700; each further Space event in the hold counts (`n`): at `WARM` 2 the line says `Keep holding…`; at `START` 5 (or `HOLD_MS` with no key repeat) the typed space is taken out (`untype()`: only when the caret still stands right after it) and listening starts. Key repeat never types. Space up, another key, the box or window losing the keyboard before that: the space stays, the line goes. Keys typed while listening go in as typed, except Esc, which cancels the take (§ Esc during a take). A failed start (`fail`) leaves the note; the rest of the hold types nothing.
- Listening (`listen()`): `api().dictation("ready")` and `getUserMedia({audio})` at once; `capture()`: an `AudioContext` at 16 kHz (its own rate when refused; the server reads any), an `AudioWorklet` `tap` loaded from a Blob (a `ScriptProcessor` when that fails) posting 100 ms batches; every sample kept (`me.pcm`, grows), its level per `FRAME_MS` 20 ms frame (`me.lv`, RMS) and those levels counted in `BINS` 50 bins of 2 dB (`me.hist`); `wavOf(me, from, to)` makes any stretch a 16-bit mono WAV (base64). No `MediaRecorder`: its WebM chunks after the first are not files of their own. A five-bar level meter (`.lvl`, the last frame's level, eased down) on the line. Server `starting`: `Starting Voice to Text…` until `api().dictation("wait")` answers; up: a red pulsing dot, `Listening… release Space to stop.`, the meter, the box's rule and ring red (`.listening`). Let go before the microphone was up: nothing is sent.
- Text while speaking (whisper_streaming's "LocalAgreement"; `planPass()`, `agreeStep()`, `closeStep()`, `alignAt()`, `finalPlan()`, `quietLine()`, `quietest()`, pure, run in node by `test-reply.py`): `pump()` runs on every batch while the server is up and no pass is out (one at a time, never queued, always up to the newest sound). The open piece is the sound from the cut (`me.cut`, a frame). `planPass()`: a frame is speech at or above `quietLine(hist)` = max(`QUIET_MIN` 0.004, min(10th percentile × `QUIET_FLOOR_X` 3.2, 90th percentile × `QUIET_SPEECH_X` 0.3)), percentiles so one knock does not turn speech into quiet. No speech for `SKIP_MS` 1 s: the cut moves to `QUIET_WIN` 300 ms before the end (`skip`). A pause (`PAUSE_MS` 500 of quiet after `SPEECH_MS` 300 of speech, starting at least `MIN_CLOSE_MS` 3 s into the piece) closes the piece `PAUSE_MS`/2 into it (`close`); no such pause in `MAX_PIECE_MS` 20 s: close at the middle of the quietest `QUIET_WIN` after half of it. Otherwise a `part` pass over the whole piece once `FIRST_MS` 2.5 s passed since its speech began (shorter first pieces of Turkish made the server loop for 5 to 16 s), with speech newer than the last part pass reached: every `TICK_MS` 1.5 s while speech goes on (not `HUSH_MS` 200 ms into a quiet spell, so a coming pause finds the server free), and at once `END_MS` 300 ms into a quiet spell that does not close the piece, so letting go soon after often needs no pass.
- A part pass's words (`agreeStep()`): after the piece's committed words (`alignAt()`: as many as were committed, or one or two either side where its word matches the last committed one), the prefix it shares with the last part pass (`wordKey()`: lower case, letters and digits only), less its own last word (cut at the edge of the sound), is committed; the rest is interim. A closing pass (`closeStep()`): every word after the committed ones is committed; the next piece starts empty. Committed words go in at the caret by `putDictation()` (`joinDictation()`, below) and are never rewritten; drafts and undo see only them. Interim words show dim at the caret (`ghost()`: a `.ghost` copy of the box, same font, padding, borders and width, draws its text with them in a `.tail` at opacity 0.65; the box's own text is clear, `.ghosted` (set before the copy reads the box's style, so it takes the padding without the quick answers' room), its caret and selection still drawn; the box grows to the copy's height; drawn again when the user types meanwhile). The placeholder is not the box's text, so `color: transparent` leaves it drawn: it is made transparent too for the whole take (`.listening`, `.ghosted`, `.busy` while transcribing: `setBusy()`); before 2026-11-25 it was drawn under the dim words of an empty box, both unreadable.
- Passes: `api().dictate(wav, "audio/wav", kind, before)`; `kind` `part` goes without the term list (with it, a few seconds of Turkish cut mid-word made the server loop for 6 to 32 s and answer garbage, `trials.md` 2026-11-24), `close` and `whole` with it; the words the hold committed before this piece (`me.before`, set when a piece closes) go as context; never the piece's own committed words, which made the recogniser leave them out of its answer (found in the timing runs: "merge the" then "pull request…" lost). A pass that fails or takes over `SLOW_MS` 6 s ends passes for the hold (`me.live`); letting go then sends the rest in one pass, as before.
- Let go (Space up, or the box or window losing the keyboard; `finish()`): `Transcribing…` (pulsing grey; it stays when the box loses the keyboard, `busy`); the worklet's last batch is asked for (`flushTap()`, 150 ms at most), the microphone stops, a pass still out is waited for (the server runs one at a time anyway). `finalPlan()`: nothing said since the cut: done; the last part pass reached `TAIL_MS` 200 ms past the last speech: its words are the final ones, no pass; else one `close` pass over the rest (`whole` when the hold committed nothing yet, so an empty answer is still "Nothing was heard"). Then the dim words go and the line goes; focus stays in the box. Dictated text goes in at the caret by `joinDictation()` (pure, node-tested, Claude Code's rule: a space before it unless the text before is empty or ends in whitespace, one after it unless the text after is empty or starts with whitespace; the caret after the words), through `insertText` when the box has the keyboard (focus stays), else into its value. A box redrawn meanwhile is found again by its key (`boxOf()`).
- Notes (amber line, no dialog; kept until the next key in that box or the box loses the keyboard; a press on the link is `preventDefault`ed on `mousedown`, so the note stays; the click calls `ReplyApi.settings(uri)` → `open_settings(uri, dictation_app)`, which opens only `SETTINGS_LINKS` or `APP_LINK`, after `AllowSetForegroundWindow(ASFW_ANY)`). Microphone (page, `micNote()` by the error's name): `NotAllowedError`/`SecurityError` "Windows blocks the microphone…" → Settings › Privacy › Microphone; `NotFoundError`/`OverconstrainedError` "No microphone found…" → Settings › System › Sound; `NotReadableError`/`AbortError` "The microphone could not start; another program may hold it…" → Settings › System › Sound. Server (`agent-reply.py`): not running and no `dictation_server` (`DICTATE_NOT_RUNNING` + "Start it, then hold Space again."), did not answer within `DICTATE_START_WAIT` 30 s of its start (`DICTATE_NOT_STARTED`), both ending "Open Voice to Text to see why, then hold Space again." with that link when `dictation_app` is set; an error answer (`DICTATE_FAILED` with the server's `error`); an empty text (`DICTATE_EMPTY` "Nothing was heard. Hold Space and speak again.").
- `Dictation` (the panel's `self.dictation`, `Dictation(local, save_local)`): `ready()` = `GET <url>/api/health` 200 (1 s) → `{up}`; else, when `dictation_server` exists, `start_hidden()` once per `DICTATE_START_WAIT` (a `.py`/`.pyw` with the `pythonw.exe` beside the panel's Python, in its folder, detached, no window; the launcher is not used, it opens a browser) → `{starting}`; `wait()` polls health every 0.25 s until up or 30 s after the start; `transcribe(bytes, mime, kind, context)` starts or waits when down, then `POST <url>/api/dictate?language=<lang>&prompt=<…>` (`dictate_query()`: the terms joined by ", " (none for `kind` `part`), then ". " and the last `DICTATE_CONTEXT` 200 characters of `context`; no `prompt` when both are empty), body the audio, `Content-Type` its mime, timeout `DICTATE_WAIT` 120 s; answer `{text}` (stripped) or a note; an empty text is the `DICTATE_EMPTY` note only for `kind` `whole`, else `{"text": ""}`.
- Settings (`claude-plus.local.json`, read at every call): `dictation` (`claude` default, or `whisper`; above), `dictation_url` (default `DICTATE_URL` `http://127.0.0.1:8765`), `dictation_server` (start script), `dictation_app` (what the note's link opens: a `.py`/`.pyw` started hidden, else `os.startfile`), `dictation_language` (default `tr`), `dictation_terms` (list; written once with `DICTATE_TERMS`, common English developer words and no private names, the first time it is missing; never overwritten). The maintainer's: the server `app.py` and its `launcher.pyw` in the Voice to Text folder.
- Esc: § Esc during a take.
- Microphone in WebView2: a page given as a string is `about:blank`, not a secure context (`navigator.mediaDevices` undefined), so the reply window loads `agent-reply.html` from its file (`file:///…`, a secure context); WebView2 asks its own Allow/Block question on every `getUserMedia` (private mode remembers nothing), so `allow_microphone(REPLY_TITLE)` (after `quiet_forms()`, on the form class) adds a `PermissionRequested` handler on that window's `CoreWebView2` answering Allow for `Microphone` alone. Windows' own microphone privacy switch for desktop apps still applies (`NotAllowedError`).
## Esc during a take
- Asked 2026-11-25 ("Aynı Claude terminalinde olduğu gibi"). A take is live from `listen()` (`takes`, by the box's key, so a box redrawn meanwhile is found again; `liveTake()`: `rec` is it, or its box is `busy`) until its text is in. Esc then (`escape()` → `cancelTake()`): nothing the take heard goes in; the microphone stops; the Claude path asks `voice("drop", "cancelled with Esc")`, so the hidden Claude Code lets go and empties its prompt once its text settles (`Dictation.drop()`); the Whisper path's passes still out land nowhere (`land()`, `finish()`, `toWhisper()`, `failed()` check `me.cancelled`); the dim words and the line go; words already committed (Voice to Text commits while held) are taken out by putting back the box as the take found it (`me.orig`, caret `me.at`, as an edit, so Ctrl+Z has them; keys typed during that take go with them); the box keeps the keyboard; Space still down: its repeats and key-up do nothing.
- At `Keep holding…` (or before it, Space still down): Esc takes the press's space out (`untype()`) and drops the hold as another key would (`drop:let go before listening`).

## Voice host (the Claude path)
- `agent-voice-host.py` (installed beside the panel, loaded by `load_voice()`; never calls Anthropic directly). `VoiceHost`: `claude` in a ConPTY (pywinpty) 120 x 40, cwd `%LOCALAPPDATA%\claude-plus\voice-host` (`HOST_DIR`, the panel's `VOICE_DIR`), `clean_env()` as the panel's, flags `--tools "" --strict-mcp-config --model haiku --settings <HOST_DIR>\voice-host-settings.json` (`SETTINGS`: `voice` enabled, `hold`, `autoSubmit` false; `disableAllHooks`, so no hook and no status line run in it). Output fed to a pyte screen (`UNKNOWN` sequences dropped first, `PARTIAL` kept for the next read; SGR 2 kept as italics). `typed()` = the prompt's text without dim cells (placeholder) or the level meter, None when no prompt is drawn; `status()` = Claude Code's voice line (`keep holding…`, `listening…`, `Transcribing…`, `No speech detected.`); `key()` refuses CR/LF; `clear_line()` = Ctrl+E, Ctrl+U three times; `stop()` = clear, Ctrl+C twice (Claude Code removes its `~/.claude/sessions/<pid>.json`), then a forced kill of the tree. pywinpty and pyte are user packages (`install.py`); missing (`MISSING`), every take is Whisper's.
- Life (`Dictation`, the panel's `self.voice`; `keep_voice()` every live pass): started once the reply window has shown (`reply_shown`) while the mode is Claude, or at the first hold; kept warm; died or its screen unreadable: started again after `RESTART_GAPS` 30, 60, 120, 300, 900, 1800 s (back to 30 after `STEADY_S` 300 s up); a start kills first any live `claude` whose session file names `HOST_DIR` (`host_pids()`, one left by a forced panel); stopped by the panel's `quit()` and when the mode turns Whisper. One instance. `running()` leaves out every session whose `cwd` is `VOICE_DIR` (`is_voice_dir()`), so it is never a card, a tab or an unknown session forcing a scan. Nothing is ever submitted: no transcript, no `~/.claude/projects` folder.
- A take (`ReplyApi.voice(step)`; page `askHold()`, `listen()`, `claudeTake()`, `claudeDone()`, `toWhisper()`): the hold rule is the same `spaceRule()` (a first Space types a space, `Keep holding…`, listening; a tap stays a space). At its `warm` the page asks `hold` → `Dictation.hold()`: `{ok}` (a thread sends a Space every `RATE_S` 33 ms, the first only after `GUARD_S` 0.6 s from the last release, since Claude Code's double tap submits; it waits up to `SLOT_WAIT_S` 2 s for the last take's prompt to be emptied) or `{whisper: why}`. At `start` the page takes the typed space out and opens its own microphone; then `read` every `VOICE_READ_MS` 100 ms → `{phase, text, note, why}`: `warm`, `listening` (the line `Listening… release Space to stop.`, the meter from the page's recording), `transcribing` (after `release` on key-up: `Transcribing…`), `done` (final: no voice word shown and the text unchanged `SETTLE_S` 0.35 s, at least `RELEASE_MIN_S` 0.3 s after the release), `error`. The prompt's words show dim at the caret (`ghost()`) while held; `done` puts the final text in at the caret (`putDictation()`, `joinDictation()`'s spaces). `No speech detected.` (any of `QUIET_NOTES`) is `done` with a `note`, shown as the amber line. Every take ends with the hidden prompt emptied (`_clear()`, after the text settles when the take was dropped or failed). The page stops reading → the Spaces stop after `BEAT_S` 1.5 s; a hold over `MAX_HOLD_S` 300 s is let go. Let go at `Keep holding…` (`cold`) or before `hold` answered: `drop`, the one space stays.
- Fallback to Whisper, for that take, logged with why: `hold` answers `whisper` (mode Whisper, host not started or starting or restarting, dead, no prompt line, packages missing, last take not emptied in 2 s); `read` answers `error`: the host died, its prompt line is gone, a new segment of the voice line holds one of `ERROR_WORDS` (`failed`, `error`, `not available`, `sign in`, `microphone`, …; segments already shown before the take, `base_of()`, are left out), no `listening…` within `LISTEN_WAIT_S` 2.5 s of the first Space (measured 0.2 to 0.4 s), or no final text `FINAL_WAIT_S` 15 s after the release. Still held: `Starting Voice to Text…`, then the Whisper take goes on live from the page's recording of the whole hold (`whisperTake()`). Already let go: the page's recording of the whole hold goes as one `whole` pass; with no recording, the microphone note.
- `voice.log` (`~/.claude/state/agent-panel/`, cut as `links.log`): the host's start, ready time, death, restart; each take's timings (listening, first words, held, end after the release), Spaces sent, characters, note or error; every fallback's reason. Never the text.
- Measured (`trials.md`, 2026-11-25): prompt 0.8 to 4 s after spawn; with no first gap, `keep holding…` 0.05 s and `listening…` 0.2 to 0.4 s after the first Space; words in the prompt while held, about every 0.2 s; final 0.3 to 0.8 s after release. Voice mode was on already (`~/.claude/settings.json` `voice`), no sign-in asked.
- `tools/voice-host-probe.py [seconds]`: the check without a window. `tools/voice-trial.pyw`: the phase 1 window *Konuşma denemesi* (Desktop shortcut `Konuşma denemesi.lnk`), its own hidden Claude Code beside the panel's; each hold appended to `HOST_DIR\trial.log` with its text. Both load the module through `tools/voice_host_load.py` and run without `-I`.

## Limits
- Dictation (Space held in an answer box): the Claude path needs Claude Code's voice mode and sign-in (the sound goes to Anthropic as from any terminal), pywinpty and pyte, and keeps one more `claude` running; not yet tried with real speech in the reply window, nor beside the page's own recording. The Whisper path needs a local server with the `/api/dictate` contract; its words come in steps of one pass (2.5 to 4 s on the CPU). Both need a microphone Windows lets desktop apps use. A key repeat delay over `HOLD_MS` 700 ms starts on the timer.
