## Behaviour
- Session panel (2026-11-07): sessions are the `<uuid>.jsonl` files directly under `~/.claude/projects/<folder>/` (subagent and set-aside transcripts excluded), plus the same under each WSL distro that is already running (`wsl -l --running`; the panel never starts one). Title: the last `custom-title` (`/rename`), else the last `ai-title`, else the first real prompt. A session must be resumed from the folder its transcript is filed under, and a session that moved (`/cd`) lists several `cwd`s, so the panel picks the `cwd` whose slug (every non-alphanumeric character as `-`) equals the folder name. Open sessions come from `~/.claude/sessions/<pid>.json` (`sessionId`, `status` busy/idle/shell, `name`) when that pid is alive. Parsed results are cached in `~/.claude/state/agent-panel/cache.json` by mtime and size; a rescan every 15 s takes about 0.05 s.
- The panel was called "session panel" (`session-panel.pyw`) until it became the Agent Panel on 2026-11-07, at the maintainer's request. The installers stop a copy under either name and delete the old file, sign-in entry and state folder.
- Right-click menu (2026-11-07, the maintainer's spec): Hide / Unhide, Close (open sessions only), Delete. The menu is HTML inside the panel (a web page cannot draw outside its window), opened at the pointer and kept inside the panel; Delete is red on a pale red hover. It closes on Escape, on a click outside (that click does nothing else), on scroll and on focus loss. The native menu was rejected (the maintainer, 2026-11-08: it ignores the theme). Hidden ids live in `~/.claude/state/agent-panel/hidden.json`; Hidden sessions, sessions whose folder is gone and headless runs (`entrypoint` `sdk-cli` in the transcript: a `claude -p` that another session started, e.g. tech news calling the Topview MCP; 2026-11-08, the maintainer) stay out of the main list and the header count; a headless card says `headless` and its menu has no Hide; the quiet link *Hidden, Deleted and Headless (n)* (it said *Show Hidden, ...* until 2026-11-13, when the Status button joined its row and the long wording no longer fitted beside it at 280 px: 197 px of text against 181) in the strip under the list adds a HIDDEN, DELETED AND HEADLESS section after the main list and scrolls to it. Cards there are not greyed; their second line says `hidden` or `folder deleted` (the maintainer, 2026-11-07: greyed cards inside the main list read as clutter; the user's word is "deleted", not "missing"). Open/working dots are refreshed every 2 s from `~/.claude/sessions/<pid>.json` alone (the full transcript scan stays at 15 s), so a session closed in its own terminal loses its dot within 2 s. Close and Delete confirm in two steps beside the control, per `mindmap/table.md` *Delete confirmation* (2026-11-08, replacing two modal dialogs): the menu turns into a small card with the question, one sentence on what happens, Cancel (focused, the default) and *Yes, Close* / *Yes, Delete*, placed under the card (above it near the bottom), never on it. Delete on an open session closes it first and waits up to 10 s for its process to end (`close_delete`); on a session stored inside WSL it is shown greyed and says why under itself (a network path has no Recycle Bin). It then moves the transcript, its `<id>/` folder and `file-history/<id>/` to the Recycle Bin with `SHFileOperationW` (`FOF_ALLOWUNDO`). Project files and the project's auto memory are never touched. Close sends `WM_CLOSE` to the session's window when no other open session shares it, otherwise ends the process; the transcript stays.
- Tested 2026-11-07: work area back to the full 1920 px with the panel running; a maximised window spans the full width and covers the panel; Ctrl+Alt+C raised the panel over it.
- Panel look (2026-11-08): the Spark theme (`mindmap/design_themes/spark_theme`) as CSS in `agent-panel.html`: Spark colours, 14 px cards with the resting `--shadow`, 10 px search box with a blue focus ring, blue hover border, colour-matched glow on the confirm buttons, blue to cyan logo tile as inline SVG; gold only for rewards. State (the maintainer, 2026-11-09, replacing a blinking dot): the dot is green for a working or open session, grey for a closed one, red for one asking. The whole card of a working session breathes: its background and a ring fade to green and back every 2.4 s (a plain blink of the dot was not seen from afar; a hard blink of whole cards, several at once, was rejected as too loud at the screen edge). A session whose own window shows a prompt waiting for an answer (a permission question or a dialog) pulses red, faster (1.2 s), so it stands out among working ones. Under reduced motion the card keeps a still green or red tint and ring, and the working dot is a hollow ring. The red state comes from `waitingFor`: Claude Code writes it into `~/.claude/sessions/<pid>.json` while `status` says `busy` (older versions) or `waiting` (2.1.319, seen 2026-11-10), and the panel reads it every 2 s; a `waiting` status without `waitingFor` counts as asking too, and any non-empty status counts as open. Before this such a session looked as if it were working. Fonts are Segoe UI (Manrope and Inter are not installed; bundling them was offered, not chosen yet). The Tk version (2026-11-07) had to replace shadows with borders and draw icons with PIL; that is why it was ported (see `mindmap/claude-plus-fa.md`, Decisions).
- Alert (2026-11-09, the maintainer: "an alert over everything, and a knock, when an agent waits for our approval"): `agent-alert.html` in a second pywebview window, made hidden at start (`on_top`, `focus=False`, frameless, `WS_EX_TOOLWINDOW | WS_EX_NOACTIVATE`), shown top centre of the monitor holding the foreground window, rounded by a window region, sized from its rows (93 + 36 a row, 4 rows then "and n more"). `check_alerts()` runs after every live pass and scan: a session that waits joins once per wait (one that stops and asks again is announced again); while its own window is in front it is held back, not dropped, and is announced as soon as that window leaves the front if it still waits (a terminal counts only when its title shows the session's name, as for tabs), and plays `mascot/knock.wav` (`tools/knock-sound.py`, three full, deep synthesized knocks half a second apart, 1.3 s) through `winsound` asynchronously; one that no longer waits leaves; the alert hides when none is left. A click on a row brings that window forward and drops the row; the cross clears the alert. Tray menu: Alerts and Alert Sound, kept in `~/.claude/state/agent-panel/options.json`. Traps: a pywebview window shown by Win32 `ShowWindow`/`SWP_SHOWWINDOW` stays blank white (WebView2 draws only when pywebview's `show()` made it visible); a hidden pywebview window left at quit keeps the process alive, so `quit()` destroys it. Not over exclusive full-screen games (Windows allows nothing there); the knock still plays. Tested with fake sessions fed through `running()` and `scan()`: one knock per new wait, row click, re-ask, cross, never the foreground window. Fix (2026-11-10, the maintainer: technews sat at a permission prompt and no alert came after the panel restarted): `check_alerts()` marked every waiting session as announced on every pass, even when `show_alert()` returned early because the alert was not ready, and pywebview makes the second window on its own thread while `main()` runs (measured: first pass at 0.59 s, alert window not yet there, its page ready at 0.99 s), so a session already waiting at start was never shown; one in front when it began waiting was likewise never shown later. Now nothing is marked announced before the page says `ready()`, the alert's window is looked up in `alert_up()` at that moment rather than at `main()`'s start, and only sessions actually announced are marked. Checked with a unit test of `check_alerts()` / `running()` against a fake USERPROFILE (startup, in front then away, re-ask, cross, `waiting` status): three cases failed before, all passed after; after the reinstall the technews alert showed.

## Limits
- Windows only (WebView2, cmd).
- A short 429 that ends a turn also triggers the dialog.
- The old window stays open; close it yourself. The way back leaves it too: its history stops where the limit hit.
- The way back needs the Agent Panel running; it is answered by mouse only (the dialog never takes the keyboard).
- Every project can fall back, so sensitive code can go to z.ai if you click Yes.
- Background badge: no runs inside WSL; subagents read right only on versions that write `finished` (2.1.323 does).
- Status dot: up to 3 minutes behind status.claude.com, and it says only what Anthropic has posted there.
- Update repair acts only on a failure Claude Code recorded; a `claude.exe` broken without a failed record is not noticed. It needs the Agent Panel running.
